Meet Focus Tracker

Welcome! Before tracking starts, we want you to know exactly what this extension does, what it never does, and how your privacy is protected. Please read the information below before deciding.

What We Track
  • Leaving Google Meet: Only that you switched away from your Meet tab or minimized the window.
  • When: The timestamp when you left and when you returned.
  • How long: The total time (seconds/minutes) you spent away from Meet.
  • Your Details: The name and meeting code you provided in the popup.
What We NEVER Track
  • No tab titles or URLs: We never know what other websites you visit.
  • No other apps: We cannot see any other desktop programs or files.
  • No screen content: We never capture screenshots or keystrokes.
  • No audio or video: We never record your microphone, webcam, or speakers.

Where does it run? Exclusively on meet.google.com. It is completely inactive on any other website.

Who can see this data? Only your teacher or instructor, who views your focus count and away time on the teacher dashboard for this meeting.
